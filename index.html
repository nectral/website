How it works

Inspect at the keyboard. Decide by policy. See it all centrally.

1. Inspect in the browser

The extension reads prompts, pastes and uploads on AI sites the moment they happen, before anything is submitted.

2. Enforce the right action

Block, redact, warn with a reason, or allow and log. Per data type, per team, per AI app.

3. Report to one console

Every event lands in a central console with user, app and data type, ready for audit and your SIEM.
